### Task 3: Basic Math
Calculate storage for 1 hour video:
- 1080p at 5 Mbps: ___
- 720p at 2.5 Mbps: ___
- 480p at 1 Mbps: ___

---

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

**1080p:** 5 Mbps × 3600s / 8 = 2.25 GB
**720p:** 2.5 × 3600 / 8 = 1.125 GB
**480p:** 1 × 3600 / 8 = 0.45 GB

Total for adaptive: ~4 GB per hour of content.

</details>
